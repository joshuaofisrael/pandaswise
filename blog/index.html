Each post answers one specific panda question in the first paragraph, then explains the evidence. We publish only when we have something more useful or better sourced than what is already out there.

Do red pandas live alone?

27 October 2026

Yes. Wild red pandas live alone for most of the year and meet mainly to mate. What GPS tracking and field studies show about their social lives.

How much do giant pandas sleep?

26 October 2026

Giant pandas eat for 10 to 16 hours a day and sleep most of the rest, in naps of 2 to 4 hours. What GPS collar studies reveal about when pandas rest.

How long do pandas live?

26 October 2026

Giant pandas live about 15 to 20 years in the wild and around 30 in zoos; the record is 38. Red pandas can reach 23. What limits a panda's lifespan.

What do red pandas eat?

25 October 2026

Red pandas eat mostly bamboo leaf tips and shoots, plus some fruit, roots, insects and the odd egg. What field and genetic studies show about their diet.

How strong is a giant panda's bite?

23 October 2026

Giant pandas have one of the strongest bites of any bear for their size. How their skull, jaw muscles and molars crush bamboo, with the research behind it.

Why are red pandas endangered?

21 October 2026

Red pandas are Endangered because their mountain forests are cut, grazed and split by roads, and because they are trapped and poached. What studies show.

How do giant pandas communicate?

20 October 2026

Giant pandas live mostly alone, so they talk through scent marks and calls. How handstand marking, scent and bleats carry messages, with the research.

Are red pandas nocturnal?

19 October 2026

Red pandas are not strictly nocturnal. They are mostly crepuscular, busiest at dawn and dusk, and awake about 45% of the day. What studies show.

Why do giant pandas eat bamboo if they are carnivores?

18 October 2026

Giant pandas belong to the order Carnivora yet live on bamboo. What genome, taste gene, gut microbe and energy studies reveal about this unusual diet.

How long is a giant panda pregnancy?

16 October 2026

Giant panda pregnancy lasts about 90 to 180 days, but after delayed implantation the fetus grows for only about 42 days. What hormone studies show.

How do red pandas stay warm?

15 October 2026

Red pandas stay warm with dense fur, furry soles and a bushy tail, by curling into a ball and by burning more energy in winter. What studies show.

Why are baby pandas so small?

14 October 2026

A newborn giant panda weighs about 3 to 5 ounces, roughly 1/900th of its mother. Skeletal scans suggest a short gestation after implantation explains why.

How big is a red panda's home range?

13 October 2026

GPS collars put the median red panda home range at about 1.4 square km. Males use more space than females, and roads and forest loss change how they roam.

Do pandas have thumbs?

12 October 2026

Giant pandas have a false thumb: an enlarged wrist bone that grips bamboo. How it works, how old it is, and why red pandas have one too.

Why are red pandas red?

8 October 2026

Red pandas are red because the russet coat blends with reddish brown moss and white lichen in their forest canopy. How the coat, mask, belly and tail work.

Why are giant pandas black and white?

8 October 2026

Why giant pandas are black and white: the leading study points to snow and shade camouflage for the body and signalling for the ears and eye patches.

How much bamboo does a giant panda eat a day?

8 October 2026

A giant panda eats roughly 12 to 45 kg of bamboo a day. Why sources disagree, how shoots vs leaves change the number, and how red pandas compare.

Do pandas hibernate?

8 October 2026

Giant pandas do not hibernate: a bamboo diet cannot build the fat reserves it needs. How they cope with winter, and how red pandas save energy.

Are red pandas related to giant pandas?

8 October 2026

Red and giant pandas are only distant relatives: one is a bear, the other the last of its own family. What DNA shows, and why both have a false thumb.

Are giant pandas still endangered?

8 October 2026

No: the IUCN has listed giant pandas as Vulnerable since 2016. What that means, the latest wild population figures and what still threatens them.
