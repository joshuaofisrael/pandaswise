Where do giant pandas live in the wild?

Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026 · Last reviewed 13 November 2026 · By the PandasWise team

Wild giant pandas live only in China, in cool, misty bamboo forests on six mountain ranges across Sichuan, Shaanxi and Gansu provinces. They once lived on lower ground, but farming and development pushed them into the mountains.

Only in China, in three provinces

WWF UK says giant pandas live in remote, high altitude mountain bamboo forests in China, in six mountain ranges across three provinces: Sichuan, Shaanxi and Gansu. The Smithsonian's National Zoo describes the same area as a few mountain ranges in south central China.

The ESABII Data Portal, a biodiversity database run by Japan's Biodiversity Center, names the six ranges as Minshan, Qinling, Qionglai, Liangshan, Daxiangling and Xiaoxiangling. It says about 75 percent of the panda population lives in Sichuan.

What the habitat looks like

The National Zoo says pandas live in broadleaf and coniferous forests with a dense understory of bamboo, at elevations between 5,000 and 10,000 feet, and that these forests are marked by torrential rains or dense mist through the year and are often wrapped in heavy cloud.

The ESABII portal gives the elevation range as 1,200 to 4,100 meters above sea level, more typically 1,500 to 3,000 meters. Pandas depend on stands of bamboo, so the type and density of bamboo shapes where they go.

Moving up and down the mountain

Giant pandas do not hibernate, but the ESABII portal says they generally move to lower elevations in winter. It adds that they may take temporary shelter in hollow trees, rock crevices and caves. Our article on whether pandas hibernate covers this in more detail.

Why they live only in the mountains

The National Zoo says pandas once lived in lowland areas, but farming, forest clearing and other development now restrict them to the mountains. The ESABII portal describes the six mountain ranges as isolated from one another, which means panda populations in different ranges are separated. Our guide to whether giant panda habitat is shrinking looks at how that has changed.

Are there pandas outside China?

Wild pandas live only in China. Pandas in zoos in other countries are part of breeding and research programs, and they are not wild populations. Red pandas are a different species with a wider range across the Himalayas, which you can compare in our giant panda vs red panda guide.
