Bamboo Breakfast

Giant pandas spend most of their waking day eating. Help this one catch as much bamboo as it can in 30 seconds. Move with the arrow keys, the A and D keys, or by dragging on the game.

Score 0 · Best 0

What this game teaches

  • About 99% of a giant panda's diet is bamboo, and it eats every above ground part of the plant.
  • Pandas spend roughly 10 to 16 hours a day feeding, because bamboo gives them very little energy.
  • A 2015 study in Science (Nie and colleagues) found giant pandas use exceptionally little energy each day, which helps them live on bamboo.
