Which Panda Is It?

Giant pandas and red pandas share a name, a bamboo diet and a wrist bone "false thumb", but they are very different animals. Read each fact and pick giant panda, red panda or both.

What this game teaches

  • The red panda is not a bear: it is the only living member of its own family, Ailuridae.
  • The giant panda is listed as Vulnerable and the red panda as Endangered on the IUCN Red List.
  • Both eat mostly bamboo and both have a "false thumb", an enlarged wrist bone used to grip stems.
