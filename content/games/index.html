Two quick, free games that teach real panda facts. They work with a mouse, a keyboard or a touch screen, they have no sound, and your best score is saved only in your own browser. There is no sign up and nothing is collected.

Bamboo Breakfast

Steer a hungry giant panda and catch falling bamboo for 30 seconds. Learn why pandas eat for up to 16 hours a day.

Which Panda Is It?

Read a fact and decide: giant panda, red panda, or both? Ten rounds, each with a sourced explanation.

Games are original works made for PandasWise. All code and art were made for PandasWise; no outside sprites, sounds or game code are used.
