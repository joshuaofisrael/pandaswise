This is the sourcing and editorial policy for PandasWise. Guides are signed by the PandasWise team. That byline is the site, not a named field scientist, and we do not invent personal credentials.

Who writes

PandasWise is a free educational site about giant pandas and red pandas. It is run by a small independent company that also operates other educational sites. The legal name is on the terms page only. We are not a zoo, a university or a conservation charity, and we are not affiliated with the organisations we cite. Pages are researched and drafted with help from AI tools, then checked against published sources before they go up. The about page says the same thing in shorter form.

What counts as a source

A fact on a guide needs a source a reader can open. We prefer:

  • The IUCN Red List, with the year of the assessment, because categories change and old labels linger in other write ups.
  • Species pages from the Smithsonian's National Zoo and Conservation Biology Institute and from San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance.
  • WWF species pages.
  • Peer reviewed papers we can open, named with the authors, the year and a DOI link.
  • The zoo's, operator's or government's own page for anything about an animal living at a zoo.
  • Xinhua when the number is an official Chinese announcement. We name the official and the date of the report.

Wikipedia can help us find a paper. It is not a source on the page. We do not cite content farms, unsourced lists or viral posts. We do not invent numbers, quotations or experts. When two good sources disagree, as they do for how much bamboo a giant panda eats in a day, we show the range and say why, instead of picking a single figure.

Zoo facts

Loans, births and departures change. The where to see pandas table links each row to the official page we checked and shows the date we last verified it. We do not write about the politics of loans. If an official page does not state a detail, we leave that detail out.

Dates on a page

Published is the day the page first went live. Last updated moves only when the words or the facts change. Last reviewed is the day we rechecked the sources, including a review that confirmed the facts still held. A navigation tweak does not get a new updated date. Population figures always name the survey or the announcement, not a vague "today".

Corrections

If a page is wrong or out of date, use the contact page. We correct factual mistakes and move the updated date when the change is real.

Pictures

Pictures are one of two kinds, and the caption says which. An AI generated image is labelled as AI generated, is not a photograph of a real individual or a real event, and links to the image policy. A real photograph is credited to the photographer and the licence, and links to the image policy as well. The full list is on the photo credits page. We do not present an AI image as a news photograph.

Links

Links in a guide go to another PandasWise page or to a source. We do not ask anyone to click an advertisement, and we do not make those links look like ads. Related reading sits in the article as ordinary links with pictures, mid page and at the end.
