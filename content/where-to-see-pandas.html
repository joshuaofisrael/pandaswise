How we verify. We only include an entry after confirming the animals on an official page. We would rather show a shorter accurate list than a long stale one.   • November 2025: Beauval's adult pair returned to China, and Taman Safari's cub Rio was born on 27 November.

Countries with no giant pandas right now

Some countries that were once famous for pandas have none at the moment, including the United Kingdom (Edinburgh Zoo's pair returned in December 2023), Japan (since January 2026), Canada (Calgary Zoo's pandas returned in 2020) and Finland (Ähtäri Zoo's pair returned in November 2024). If you are in one of these countries, look for red pandas instead.

Tips for seeing pandas

  • Plan around activity. Giant pandas alternate long feeding bouts with naps of a few hours, so check the zoo's feeding or keeper talk times. Red pandas are most active at dawn and dusk.
  • Check the zoo's news page the week you go. Pandas can be moved off show for quarantine, breeding season or health checks, as Macao's twins are from 9 October 2026.
  • Expect timed entry for new arrivals. New panda exhibits often use timed tickets or queues in the first months.
  • Look up first. Red pandas and young giant pandas often rest high in trees.
