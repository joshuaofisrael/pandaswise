Google Analytics 4

With your consent, PandasWise uses Google Analytics 4, a service from Google, to understand how visitors use the site. It collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, how you arrived (for example from a search engine), your approximate location (country or city, derived from a shortened IP address), and your device, browser and screen size. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Cookies: Google Analytics sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_* that last up to two years and distinguish visitors and sessions.

Consent: Google Analytics does not load until you click Accept on the cookie banner. Before then, Google Consent Mode is set to deny analytics storage. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we do not ask on every visit.

Opting out: click Decline on the banner, or clear this site's stored data in your browser to see the banner again and change your choice. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy.
