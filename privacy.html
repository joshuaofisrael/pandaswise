Privacy policy

PandaWise is a static information website operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. You can read every page without an account, we show no ads, and we do not set tracking cookies. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, to keep the service secure and working. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Analytics

PandaWise does not currently run any analytics script. If we add one, we plan to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, and we will update this section before it goes live.

Contact form and email

If you use our contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third party form service, and delivered to our email inbox. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and message. We use these details only to reply to you and to improve the site, and we do not sell or share them.

Advertising and affiliate links

PandaWise currently has no advertising and no affiliate links. If this changes we will update this policy first.

External links

Our pages link to zoos, research papers and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

PandaWise is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the contact form.
